Complete device entries

The hub writes each mesh device and group that is not in your YAML to the log, and to the last_unconfigured_device sensor, with an empty type:. Paste these lines below (from the log or the sensor history, one or many) to fill in the type, a name and an icon from the product id. You can also paste a product id alone, like 0x32, to look it up.

This part does not use the AwoX cloud, everything stays in this browser.
